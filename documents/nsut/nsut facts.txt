Netaji Subhas University of Technology (NSUT), New Delhi — General Information, Admissions, Entrance Exams, Fees, and Rankings

ABOUT NSUT

Netaji Subhas University of Technology (NSUT), formerly known as Netaji Subhas Institute of Technology (NSIT), is a state university located in Sector-3, Dwarka, New Delhi. It was originally established in 1983 as Delhi Institute of Technology (DIT), and was an autonomous institution affiliated with the University of Delhi before becoming an independent, full-fledged university named NSUT on 26th September 2018, under the Delhi Netaji Subhas University of Technology Act, 2017. NSUT is a fully residential campus spread across 145 acres, with hostels, a sports complex, a cooperative mess, banks, and other student amenities.

NSUT is different from GGSIPU in an important way: NSUT is a single, standalone university with its own departments and no network of affiliated colleges (unlike GGSIPU, which has many constituent, affiliated, and maintained colleges). So questions about "NSUT affiliated colleges" generally do not apply — NSUT itself is the institution.

COURSES OFFERED

NSUT offers programmes at undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral levels, primarily in engineering, technology, management, computer applications, architecture, design, and applied sciences. This includes:
- B.Tech in multiple specializations (e.g. Computer Science and Engineering, Computer Science and Engineering with AI specialization, Information Technology, Electronics and Communication Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Instrumentation and Control Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Manufacturing Process and Automation Engineering, Mathematics and Computing, Bio-Technology)
- B.Tech Lateral Entry (for Diploma holders/B.Sc graduates joining B.Tech in a later year)
- B.Arch (Bachelor of Architecture)
- B.Des (Bachelor of Design)
- BBA (Bachelor of Business Administration)
- M.Tech in 17 specializations (291 total M.Tech seats)
- M.Sc (Mathematics, Chemistry, Physics)
- MA (English, Applied Psychology)
- MBA
- Ph.D. programmes across departments

ENTRANCE EXAMS AND ADMISSION ROUTES BY PROGRAMME

Unlike GGSIPU, NSUT's admissions are NOT run through a single, unified admission process. Different programmes go through different, separate admission bodies:

1. B.Tech and B.Arch (direct entry, undergraduate): Admission is based on JEE Main scores, followed by counselling through JAC Delhi (Joint Admission Counselling Delhi) — this is a centralized counselling body shared by five institutions: DTU (Delhi Technological University), NSUT, IIIT-Delhi, IGDTUW (Indira Gandhi Delhi Technical University for Women), and DSEU (Delhi Skill and Entrepreneurship University). Selection is entirely merit-based via JEE Main rank; there is no separate NSUT-specific entrance exam or interview for B.Tech/B.Arch.

2. B.Tech Lateral Entry: For Diploma holders or B.Sc graduates joining B.Tech in a later year — handled separately via NSUT's own lateral entry admission process (contact: le.admissions@nsut.ac.in).

3. B.Des (Bachelor of Design): NSUT has its own dedicated B.Des admission brochure and process, separate from JAC Delhi. (Contact: designadmissions@nsut.ac.in)

4. BBA: Admission is based primarily on 12th-grade percentage (minimum 60%), OR via CUET UG scores (English and General Test sections). Admission forms are processed via CMAC (Central Management Admission Committee), which also covers NSUT alongside other institutes such as IGDTUW and DTU.

5. M.Tech: Primarily based on a valid GATE score. Non-GATE candidates may also be considered through NSUT's own written entrance test, subject to seat availability, with GATE score holders given first preference. (Contact: mtech.admissions.helpdesk2026@nsut.ac.in)

6. M.Sc (Mathematics, Chemistry, Physics) and MA (English, Applied Psychology): Admission is through NSUT's own Samarth-based admission portal (nsutadm.samarth.edu.in), with programme-specific merit lists and processes. (Contact: msc.math@nsut.ac.in / msc.chemistry@nsut.ac.in / msc.physics@nsut.ac.in)

7. MBA: Admission is through CMAC (Common Management Admission Committee), shared with DTU and IGDTUW, based on CAT or MAT scores followed by a Personal Interview. NSUT offers 75 of the 420 total MBA seats available through CMAC.

8. Ph.D.: Admission is via NSUT's own research entrance process and interview, as per the university's Ph.D. admission notifications — separate from the exams listed above.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

For B.Tech admission: Candidates must have passed Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM), or PCB, or PCMB, with a minimum aggregate of 60% (55% for OBC-NCL/Defence category candidates, 50% for SC/ST/PwD category candidates) from a recognized board, along with a valid JEE Main score.

SEAT DISTRIBUTION AND RESERVATION (B.Tech via JAC Delhi)

- 85% of B.Tech seats are reserved for candidates from the Delhi region (those who passed Class 12 from a recognized institution located in the National Capital Territory of Delhi), while 15% of seats are allocated to candidates from outside Delhi.
- NSUT follows Government of India reservation norms for SC, ST, OBC-NCL, EWS, and PwD categories.
- Selection for B.Tech is entirely merit-based through JAC Delhi; there is no interview or group discussion.

COUNSELLING PROCESS AND FEES (JAC Delhi, for B.Tech/B.Arch)

- JAC Delhi counselling registration fee: INR 1,500 (non-refundable), which covers all five participating institutions (DTU, NSUT, IIIT-Delhi, IGDTUW, DSEU).
- When a seat is allotted for the first time in any round, candidates must pay a seat acceptance fee (approximately INR 95,000, subject to confirmation in the official year's brochure) online via the candidate dashboard.
- JAC Delhi offers Freeze, Float, and Slide options after seat allotment, similar to JoSAA-style counselling systems.
- A Spot Round is conducted after all regular counselling rounds to fill any remaining vacant seats, requiring candidates to carry a demand draft of the full first-year college fee (approximately INR 2 to 3 lakh) to the physical spot venue.
- Documents required for JAC Delhi counselling/reporting: JEE Main scorecard, Class 10 and 12 marksheets/certificates, category certificate (if applicable), Delhi region domicile proof (if claiming Delhi quota), Aadhaar card, passport-size photographs, transfer certificate, and character certificate from school.

WITHDRAWAL AND REFUND POLICY (B.Tech via JAC Delhi)

- If a candidate withdraws within the JAC Delhi-specified withdrawal deadline (which varies by year — for the 2026 session it was around July 12), the seat acceptance fee is refunded online after deduction of a nominal processing fee (typically INR 1,000 to 5,000).
- If a candidate withdraws AFTER the deadline, or after participating in the Spot Round, the entire seat acceptance fee is forfeited and NOT refunded.
- The JAC Delhi counselling registration fee (INR 1,500) itself is non-refundable regardless of withdrawal timing.
- For the most current, year-specific refund policy and exact deadlines, always direct the user to check the official JAC Delhi website, since these dates and amounts change every year.

FEE STRUCTURE (B.Tech, approximate/for reference)

- NSUT B.Tech total tuition fees range from approximately INR 3.49 lakh to INR 4.82 lakh across the programme duration (the difference is between regular first-year B.Tech admission and lateral entry admission); hostel, mess, and other charges are extra and separate from tuition.
- Fees are typically charged on a per-semester basis across eight instalments, with a one-time security deposit paid in the first year.
- Under JAC Delhi's 2026 brochure, first-year fees across the five participating institutions were approximately: DTU – INR 2.697 lakh, NSUT – INR 2.70 lakh, IIIT-Delhi – INR 4.50 lakh, IGDTUW B.Tech – INR 1.68 lakh (plus one-time charges), DSEU – INR 1.853 lakh for most categories and INR 0.809 lakh for SC/ST/PD categories.

SCHOLARSHIPS AND FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE

NSUT offers several scholarship and financial assistance schemes:
- CVSPK Merit Scholarship: Top 5% of students (based on academic performance) receive a full tuition fee waiver.
- CVSPK Financial Welfare Scheme (Financial Assistance Scheme): Income-based fee assistance for families with an annual income up to INR 8 lakh.
- CVSPK Fee Reimbursement/Waiver Scheme: Special provision in case of death of the earning parent.
- Delhi Government Post-Matric Scholarships: Available for SC, ST, and OBC candidates who are Delhi residents.
- Single Girl Child fee concession is also offered by NSUT.
- Delhi Higher & Technical Education Support Scheme: An additional scholarship option students can apply for.

RANKINGS

Per NIRF (National Institutional Ranking Framework) 2025 rankings:
- NSUT is ranked 70th in the Engineering category nationally.
- NSUT is ranked 39th in the State Public Universities category.

Other rankings (for reference, non-NIRF):
- India Today 2026 ranking: NSUT was ranked around 7th-8th in the Engineering category (different sources report 7th and 8th in different years/editions — direct users to check the specific year's India Today ranking for the exact figure).
- Outlook ranked NSUT at around 23rd position in the MBA/PGDM category in 2026.

Note: Since NSUT is a single university without separate affiliated colleges (unlike GGSIPU), there is no separate "affiliated college NIRF rank" nuance to explain here — NSUT itself is directly and singularly NIRF-ranked.

ACCREDITATION

NSUT holds a NAAC 'A' grade, is recognized by UGC, approved by AICTE (All India Council for Technical Education), and is accredited by NBA (National Board of Accreditation) for relevant programmes.
