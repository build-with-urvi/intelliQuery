Domino's Pizza India — General Information and FAQs

This document covers general Domino's Pizza India policies, delivery information, payment methods, and customer service details, compiled from publicly available secondary sources (not Domino's own official internal documentation). Treat specific figures (fees, exact timings) as representative and verify against official current sources before relying on them for anything important.

DELIVERY GUARANTEE

Domino's India is known for its "30 minutes or free" delivery guarantee — if a delivery takes longer than 30 minutes from when the order is placed, the order may be provided free of charge, subject to conditions. Important exceptions to this guarantee:
- It does not apply to bulk orders (4 or more pizzas in one order).
- It does not apply on New Year's Eve, Ganesh Festival, Christmas, and Durga Puja.
- It may be temporarily withdrawn during difficult operating conditions (bad weather, very high order volume, etc.), which will be announced at the time the order is taken.
- Domino's maximum liability under this guarantee is capped at Rs. 300.
- Typical delivery times generally range from 30 to 45 minutes, though this can be longer during busy periods.

PAYMENT METHODS

Domino's India generally accepts multiple payment methods, including:
- Cash on delivery
- Credit and debit cards
- Payment through the Domino's app or website (which may support UPI and digital wallets depending on region)
Customers should check the app/website at the time of ordering for the most current list of accepted payment options, as these can vary.

ORDER CANCELLATION AND REFUNDS

- Order cancellation is available for select Cash on Delivery orders placed through the Domino's app, and only within 60 seconds of placing the order. After that window, whether an order can be cancelled depends on its current status (e.g. if it has already started being prepared).
- If an eligible order is cancelled, refunds are generally processed within 5 to 7 working days to the original payment method.
- For complaints about a delivered order (wrong item, quality issue, etc.), customers are generally directed to contact customer care or use the complaint/feedback option on the app or website.
- If the order is being prepared or out for the delivery then the cancellation is not prossible 
- If the mode of payment selected is other than Cash on Delivery such as UPI or credit / Debit card then order cancelation is not possible 
- If the order was placed by any other means other than dominos app then the canccelation is not possible .

DISCOUNTS AND OFFERS

- Domino's typically allows only one coupon or promotional discount code to be applied per order.
- Offers and discounts change periodically and may include discounted pizza combos, value deals, or limited-time menu items.

DIETARY AND ALLERGEN INFORMATION

- Domino's offers a gluten-free crust option made from a gluten-free flour blend at some locations, though availability can vary by store — customers with dietary restrictions should confirm availability with their specific local store.
