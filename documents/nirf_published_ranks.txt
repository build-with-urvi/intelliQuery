NIRF Published Rankings for Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU), New Delhi

This document lists the official NIRF (National Institutional Ranking Framework) rankings published by the Ministry of Education, Government of India, for Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU). These ranks are for the university as a whole and its constituent university schools, not for individual affiliated colleges. Individual affiliated colleges of GGSIPU are generally not separately ranked by NIRF.

NIRF Rankings 2025 (most recent, published by NIRF, Ministry of Education):

- Overall (University category): Rank 93, with a score of 48.01
- State Public Universities category: Rank 22, with a score of 57.49
- Law (via University School of Law & Legal Studies, USLLS): Rank 22, with a score of 60.05
- Management (via University School of Management Studies, USMS): Rank 76, with a score of 48.88
- Architecture and Planning (via University School of Architecture and Planning, USAP): Rank 36, with a score of 51

NIRF Rankings 2024 (previous year, for reference/comparison):

- GGSIPU showed a slight change in the Law category compared to 2025. The Law category OVERALL score improved by approximately 1.37 points from 2024 to 2025.
- Overall university rank in 2024 was in a broadly similar range to 2025, with year-to-year fluctuation being normal for NIRF rankings.

Important context on how NIRF ranks GGSIPU:

NIRF (National Institutional Ranking Framework) evaluates institutions on several parameters, generally including:
- TLR: Teaching, Learning and Resources
- RP: Research and Professional Practice
- GO: Graduation Outcomes
- OI: Outreach and Inclusivity
- PR: Perception
- Additional parameters like QNR (Quality of Publications) and SFC may apply depending on category

GGSIPU is evaluated and ranked by NIRF as a single university, and separately, some of its constituent University Schools (such as USMS for Management, USLLS for Law, USAP for Architecture and Planning) receive their own category-specific ranks within their respective subject categories (Management, Law, Architecture and Planning).

Individual affiliated colleges of GGSIPU (such as engineering or management colleges affiliated with the university) are typically NOT separately evaluated or ranked by NIRF. If a student or parent asks about the NIRF rank of a specific affiliated college by name, the correct answer is that such colleges generally do not have their own separate NIRF rank — only GGSIPU as a university, and its constituent schools, are NIRF-ranked. Students should be directed to check the official NIRF website (nirfindia.org) or the specific college's website if they want to confirm whether that particular college has ever been separately ranked.

Note: NIRF rankings are updated annually (usually released around July-September each year). For the most current rankings beyond what is listed here, users should be directed to check the official NIRF website (https://www.nirfindia.org) or the official GGSIPU NIRF page (https://ipu.ac.in/nirfmain.php).
